♠ Solitaire Photos — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2025

Your photos never leave your browser.

What This App Does

Solitaire Photos is a Klondike solitaire game that optionally uses your Google Photos as card back images. The entire application runs in your browser — there is no server, no backend, and no data collection.

Google Photos Access

When you choose to connect Google Photos, the app:

  • Requests read-only access to photos you explicitly select via Google's Picker UI
  • Downloads thumbnail-sized copies of your selected photos directly to your browser
  • Converts thumbnails to local blob URLs (binary data stored in browser memory)

What We Store

Data Where Duration
OAuth access token Browser memory (JavaScript variable) Until page close or sign-out (~60 min max)
Photo thumbnails Browser memory (blob URLs) Until page close or photo change

What We Do NOT Do

  • ❌ We do not store tokens in localStorage, cookies, or URLs
  • ❌ We do not send your photos or data to any server
  • ❌ We do not use analytics, tracking, or advertising
  • ❌ We do not access photos you did not explicitly select
  • ❌ We do not retain any data after you close the browser tab

OAuth Scope

This app requests the photospicker.mediaitems.readonly scope, which only allows reading photos that you explicitly choose in Google's picker interface. It cannot browse, modify, or delete your photo library.

Third-Party Services

The only external service used is Google's Identity Services (for sign-in) and the Google Photos Picker API (for photo selection). Both are governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

Open Source

This application is open source. You can inspect the code at github.com/vbomfim/solitaire-photos.

Contact

For questions about this privacy policy, please open an issue on the GitHub repository.
